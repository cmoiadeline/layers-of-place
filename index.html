Layers of Place.

A place is not one moment. It is a stack of them.

Our question: how do historic sites and the city around them reveal change over time? We read Samarkand as three kinds of layer:

  • UnearthedAfrasiyab: the city before the city we see.
  • PreservedRegistan and Gur-e-Amir: monuments kept, restored and re-read.
  • LivedModern architecture and local customs: the layers still being made.

UNESCO itself reads the city in three zones: Afrasiyab (oldest, north-east), the Timurid city (south) and the 19th–20th-century western extension. The centre of Samarkand kept moving, and every era reused, rebuilt and re-read what it inherited.

We separate documented fact, our interpretation and open questions, and we say which is which.
